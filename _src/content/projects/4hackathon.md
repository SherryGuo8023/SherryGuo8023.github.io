In 48 hours our Dutch, Latvian and Chinese team built a predictive tool for city crowd simulation, integrating OpenStreetMap data with a crowd simulator, and took [second prize](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:6789168056292646912/) among 23 international teams.

## What we built

- Large-scale crowd simulation with more than 50,000 agents
- An OpenStreetMap importer so any city can be loaded
- Working simulations of crowd flow in Riga and Amsterdam

Meant for major-event crowd analysis, urban planning and evacuation planning. Open source.
