Accepted to the Short Papers track of BritCHI 2026, the 39th International BCS Human-Computer Interaction Conference, Brunel University of London, 1-3 November 2026.

Written with Jinghan Zhu, this short paper draws on our experience building AI character products at Kotoko AI. It separates two kinds of connection such products can support, connection *with* an AI character and connection *between people* that an AI character catalyses, and argues for designs that raise human relationships rather than replace them.
