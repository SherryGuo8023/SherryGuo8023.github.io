Accepted to the Case Studies track of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Interactive Digital Entertainment (AIIDE 2026). Presentation scheduled for November 2026.

This case study traces the path from [Dobit](/portfolio/2dobit/), a cross-platform AI companion, to [Bside](/portfolio/1bside/), a multiplayer social simulation game shipped on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3649950/Bside/), [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bside/id6757434275) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kotoko.bside). It focuses on the engineering work needed to turn open-ended language model output into actions the game can execute, and on the lessons learned across three generations of LLM-native character systems.
