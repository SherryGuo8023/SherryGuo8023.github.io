Hi\(@^0^@)/

I'm Yunjia "Sherry" Guo, a game developer and AI researcher. I'm the CTO and hands-on technical lead at [Kotoko AI](https://www.kotoko.ai/), where we build [Bside](https://www.bside.zone/), an AI-native social simulation game on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3649950/Bside/), [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bside/id6757434275) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kotoko.bside) in which the characters are driven by large language models. I led its engineering from the first prototype through international launch, across three generations of LLM-native client, server and character systems.

## Current Work
My work sits where games meet HCI: how do you keep open-ended model behaviour controllable, executable and meaningful inside a live multiplayer world? In Bside, AI characters talk to each other, act in a shared 3D world, and can be steered by players without losing their autonomy. Alongside production I publish on what we learn:

- **Bounded Autonomy: Controlling LLM Characters in Live Multiplayer Games** (first author), [arXiv:2604.04703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04703), 2026
- **Dobit to Bside: Grounding LLM Characters into Executable Gameplay** (first author), accepted at AIIDE 2026, Case Studies track
- **Raise, Don't Replace: Two Kinds of Connection in AI Character Products** (with Jinghan Zhu), accepted at BritCHI 2026, Short Papers track

See the [Publications](/publications/) page for details. In 2026 I was a finalist for the Innovator Award at the Women in Tech Awards.

## Background
Before Kotoko, I was a game developer at Tencent's TiMi Studios, building core character, control, camera and ability framework systems in Unreal Engine 4 for an AAA open-world action game, alongside Unity work. Before that I worked at NetEase Games on procedural urban generation in Houdini and on stylised rendering. My professional journey includes contributions to titles such as Once Human (PC survival game, Starry Studio) and Metal Slug: Awakening (mobile, Tencent), alongside unannounced action mobile games and AAA shooter projects.

I hold an MSc in Game and Media Technology from Utrecht University, where I specialised in procedural content generation and crowd simulation, and a BSc in Physics from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences with a minor in mathematics. My physics years were spent on superconductors: ARPES measurements at the Institute of Physics, CAS, a summer at the Max Planck Institute for Solid State Research, and a bachelor thesis on machine learning for predicting topological materials. Moving from physics to game technology reflects what I still care about most: combining scientific rigour with creative work.

### Philosophy & Vision
I view games as an art form that pushes the boundaries of technology while delivering exceptional player experiences through gameplay and visual excellence. Games serve as natural proving grounds for AI advancement, exemplified by the groundbreaking achievements of AlphaGo and AlphaStar. The latter's mastery of StarCraft II - my favourite game, where I main Zerg and love Sarah Kerrigan - demonstrated AI's potential in handling complex, incomplete-information environments.

What truly drives me is the endless potential for innovation in gaming. From Overwatch's revolutionary sound design (which inspired my career path) to LLM-driven characters in modern games, I'm passionate about pushing the boundaries of what's possible in interactive entertainment. Games not only bridge academia and industry but continuously challenge the limits of computational capabilities and human experience.
