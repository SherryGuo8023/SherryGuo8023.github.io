Urban Mobility Hackathon

A 48-hour crowd simulation tool for city planning. Second prize among 23 teams.

  • 2021
  • Team Čupa
  • Event
  • Team submission
  • Second prize announcement
  • Riga demo
  • Amsterdam demo

In 48 hours our Dutch, Latvian and Chinese team built a predictive tool for city crowd simulation, integrating OpenStreetMap data with a crowd simulator, and took second prize among 23 international teams.

What we built

  • Large-scale crowd simulation with more than 50,000 agents
  • An OpenStreetMap importer so any city can be loaded
  • Working simulations of crowd flow in Riga and Amsterdam

Meant for major-event crowd analysis, urban planning and evacuation planning. Open source.
