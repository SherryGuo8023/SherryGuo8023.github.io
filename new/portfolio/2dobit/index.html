Dobit

An AI companion with its own personality, memory and daily life, on mobile and PC.

  • 2023 – 2024
  • Kotoko AI
  • Official site
  • YouTube review
  • Another review

Dobit is a cross-platform AI companion game that combines generative character creation with personalised LLM interaction. Each companion has a unique look, a personality and a memory, and lives in a small town alongside other characters, on your phone or as a desktop pet on PC.

How it works

Character generation. Stable Diffusion for unique character visuals, a Spine-based animation pipeline, and procedural personality generation so no two companions start the same.

LLM integration. Character-specific dialogue that keeps personality traits consistent, long-term memory so the relationship can develop, and context-aware responses that know what the character has been doing.

A living town. An ecosystem of pre-designed NPCs with distinct personalities, relationship dynamics between characters, and bonding mechanics between player and companion.

What Dobit taught us

Dobit was the first generation of the character systems that later became Bside. The road from one companion to a shipped multiplayer world is the subject of a case study accepted at AIIDE 2026; see Papers.

Built with Unity, Stable Diffusion and a custom LLM integration layer.
