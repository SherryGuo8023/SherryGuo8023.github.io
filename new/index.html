Right now: Bside

A social simulation game on Steam, iOS and Android where every character is driven by a language model. I have led its engineering from the first prototype through international launch, across three generations of client, server and character systems.

Bside

A social simulation game where the characters think for themselves. Players create characters with their own looks, backstory and personality, then watch them live, talk and act in a shared world, on PC and on their phones. The hard part, and the part I care about, is keeping open-ended model behaviour controllable, executable and meaningful inside a live multiplayer game.

The road here

  • 2022 – 2023
